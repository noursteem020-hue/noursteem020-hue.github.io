Privacy Policy

BrightBean Trial — self-hosted deployment. Effective date: 19 October 2026.

1. Who we are

BrightBean Trial ("the app") is a private, self-hosted deployment of the open-source BrightBean Studio software, operated on its owner's own computer for the owner's own social channels only. There are no other users and no public sign-up.

2. What data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through the platform authorization screens, the app may access:

  • Basic profile information of the connected accounts (name, handle, avatar).
  • YouTube channel data needed to upload videos, manage posts, synchronize comments for replies, and read analytics.
  • Posts, comments, and analytics of the owner's own channels (read and write as required for scheduling, publishing, and replying).

3. How data is stored

All data — including OAuth tokens and synchronized content — is stored in a local database on the owner's own computer. Tokens are used only to call the official platform APIs on the owner's behalf. No data is transmitted to any third party, sold, or used for advertising.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data remains on the local machine until the owner deletes it. Access can be revoked at any time from the platform side (Google account permissions, Meta Business settings), after which the corresponding tokens stop working immediately.
