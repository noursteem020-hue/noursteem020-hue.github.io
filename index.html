BrightBean Trial private use

A self-hosted social publishing assistant for its owner's own channels.

What this app is

BrightBean Trial is a private, self-hosted deployment of the open-source BrightBean Studio publishing tool. It connects — with the owner's consent — to the owner's own social accounts (YouTube, and later Facebook/Instagram) to schedule and publish posts, synchronize comments for replies, and read channel analytics. It is not a public service and has no other users.
